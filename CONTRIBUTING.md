# How to contribute

Community AI Lab is starting from zero. We're looking for engineers, researchers, people who know about audio or GPUs, and contributors interested in testing, writing or organising tasks. You don't need to attend meetings.

## Where to start

1. Open the [Community AI Lab organisation](https://github.com/Community-AI-Lab).
2. Choose [Contributor Platform](https://github.com/Community-AI-Lab/platform) or [Speech-to-Speech Model](https://github.com/Community-AI-Lab/speech-to-speech).
3. Find a task in the project's **Issues** tab, or suggest one that should be added.
4. Comment on the task to say you're interested. Share your code, notes or results in an issue or pull request so someone can review them.

Start with whatever matches your skills. The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) shows the current goals.

## Other kinds of contributions

The platform will eventually support data, audio evaluation and GPU compute. Those submission tools do **not** exist yet. Please don't send sensitive datasets, recordings, or GPU credentials through issues.

## Contributor agreement and credits

We intend to introduce a reviewed founding contributor agreement, with a separate commercial and revenue-sharing agreement later. **No CLA is currently in force through this repository.** Until legal terms are approved and accepted, do not rely on credit records as financial entitlements, and do not submit materials whose rights you cannot grant.

For early participation we can record accepted tasks, authors and review evidence as a provisional contribution log. Points, royalties, equity and payout rights are not yet defined.

## Working principles

- Start small and keep code reproducible.
- Put decisions in issues or pull requests, not private meetings.
- Avoid introducing new dependencies unless they solve a demonstrated problem.
- Never add credentials, employer-confidential material, personally identifiable information or audio without appropriate rights and consent.
- Be respectful in reviews; address the work, not the person.
